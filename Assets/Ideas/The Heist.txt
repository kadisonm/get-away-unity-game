The Heist
Style: Low poly cartoonish

Main Ideas:
- Getaway
- Timed survival to get to next level
- Levels (increasingly get longer)
- Police cars chasing
- Not touch anything (crash = lose money)
- Levels
- Different maps
- Buy new cars
- Can destroy the police cars (extra money/points)
- Power ups
- 5 minute survival time limit

3 Levels (different maps):
- House (Suburbs) - Sun rise
- Mansion (Rich neighbourhood) - Sun rise
- Bank (City) - At night

Power ups:
- Send 1 missile at a police car
- Speed up
- Invincibility power up (30 seconds)
- Teleport to random spot
